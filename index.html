繁體中文

系統與操作

支援 macOS 12 或更新版本、Intel 與 Apple silicon Mac，以鍵盤及滑鼠遊玩。

  • WASD 移動；滑鼠轉視角，滾輪縮放。
  • 左鍵攻擊、右鍵或 Shift 衝刺；Space 跳躍。
  • 按 Esc 暫停，進入「設定」可調整語言。

主角名字

第一次按「開始冒險」可以自己取名；留空就叫「毛毛」。已有進度時可到設定改名，進度會保留。「新的冒險」會開始新遊戲。

教學與冒險進度

跟著新手村的提示走到畢業考，真正擊敗史萊姆隊長後才會開放畢業傳送門。接下來到草原各區擊敗頭目、找回心之線團，逐章開啟新的傳送門。

滑鼠視角沒反應？

關閉對話、選單或繪本後，若畫面提示「點一下畫面，繼續控制視角」，請在遊戲畫面點一下，讓滑鼠重新被遊戲擷取。按 Esc 可開啟設定或返回遊戲。

回報問題

請附上遊戲版本、Mac 型號／macOS 版本、重現步驟，以及你看到的提示。若附截圖，先檢查畫面。不要附上真實姓名、聯絡方式、訂單資料、帳號資訊或個人存檔。GitHub issue 是公開頁面。

English

System and controls

Fluffland supports macOS 12 or later on Intel and Apple silicon Macs. Play with a keyboard and mouse.

  • WASD moves; move the mouse to look around and scroll to zoom.
  • Left click attacks; right click or Shift dashes; Space jumps.
  • Press Esc to pause, then open Settings to change the language.

Name your hero

The first time, press Start Adventure to choose a name. Leave it blank to use “Maomao.” If you already have progress, change the name in Settings and keep it. New Adventure starts a new game.

Tutorial and chapters

Follow the village lessons to the final exam. You must defeat Captain Slime to open the graduation portal. Then defeat area bosses and bring back the Heart Yarn to unlock later chapter portals.

Can’t turn the camera?

After closing a conversation, menu, or picture book, follow the on-screen prompt and click once inside the game to recapture the mouse. Press Esc to open Settings or return to play.

Report a problem

Please include the game version, Mac model/macOS version, steps to reproduce, and any on-screen message. Check screenshots before attaching them. Do not post your real name, contact details, order information, account information, or personal save files. GitHub issues are public.
