隱私政策 / Privacy Policy

本頁說明 Mac 版遊戲本身的資料處理，也區分購買、網站瀏覽與自願回報問題。
How the Mac game handles your data, and where storefronts, website hosting, and voluntary reports differ.

繁體中文

遊戲本身

絨絨之境 Mac 版不要求建立帳號，沒有廣告或遊戲內購，也沒有啟用分析或跨 App 追蹤。遊戲不會把你自訂的主角名字、遊戲進度或設定傳給開發者。

本機存檔

主角名字、進度、裝備與遊戲設定儲存在你的 Mac 本機。它們用於繼續冒險和保留偏好；遊戲內不提供雲端帳號或同步功能。請自行管理與備份 Mac 上的個人存檔。

商店與網站是不同服務

透過 Mac App Store 購買或下載時，Apple 依其隱私政策處理商店服務相關資料；透過 itch.io 購買或下載時，itch.io 依其隱私政策處理。這兩個平台的購買、付款、帳號及下載資料，不是由遊戲本身收集。

本支援與隱私網頁使用 GitHub Pages 發布。瀏覽網頁時，GitHub 可能依其隱私聲明處理網站服務相關資料；本頁本身不加入外部字型、圖片、腳本或追蹤碼。

自願回報問題

如果你在 GitHub issues 自願提交回報，你寫下的內容與 GitHub 帳號名稱可能公開，並由 GitHub 依其隱私聲明處理；開發者會使用你主動提供的內容來了解與處理問題。請不要上傳個人存檔、真實姓名、聯絡資訊、訂單或帳號資料。若需更正或刪除你公開的回報，請使用 GitHub 提供的編輯／刪除功能或在該回報中提出請求。

English

The game itself

Fluffland for Mac does not require an account. It has no ads or in-app purchases, and analytics and cross-app tracking are not enabled. The game does not send your chosen hero name, game progress, or settings to the developer.

Local saves

Your hero name, progress, equipment, and game settings are stored locally on your Mac so you can continue playing and keep your preferences. The game has no cloud account or sync feature. Please manage and back up your own local saves.

Storefronts and this website are separate services

If you buy or download through the Mac App Store, Apple handles store-related data under its Privacy Policy. If you buy or download through itch.io, itch.io handles that activity under its Privacy Policy. The game itself does not collect those platforms’ purchase, payment, account, or download data.

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may handle website-service data under its General Privacy Statement. These pages add no external fonts, images, scripts, or tracking code.

Voluntary problem reports

If you choose to file a report on GitHub issues, your post and GitHub username may be public and GitHub handles its service under its General Privacy Statement. The developer uses what you voluntarily provide to understand and resolve the issue. Please do not upload personal save files, your real name, contact details, order information, or account details. To correct or remove a public report, use GitHub’s edit/delete controls or ask in that issue.
